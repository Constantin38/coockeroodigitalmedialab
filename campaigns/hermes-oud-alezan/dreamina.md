# Dreamina execution brief

## Campaign
**Oud Alezan — The Memory of Skin**

Status: **UNOFFICIAL SPEC CONCEPT — NOT COMMISSIONED BY HERMÈS**

## Recommended generation workflow

Generate each shot separately rather than asking one long generation to solve the entire film.

For each shot:

1. Open the Dreamina Seedance video workspace.
2. Upload only the talent references listed in `storyboard.json` for that shot.
3. For shot 05, also upload an official Oud Alezan product packshot.
4. Paste the corresponding prompt from `prompts/`.
5. Select the closest available duration.
6. Use 16:9.
7. Prefer 1080p where available.
8. Generate 2–4 candidates per shot.
9. Review identity consistency, hand anatomy, bottle integrity and motion before selecting a take.
10. Assemble the final 23-second master in editing software.

## Audio direction

Keep sound sparse:
- low room tone
- subtle breath
- distant leather movement
- single restrained hoof resonance
- muted tactile fabric / horse-hair detail
- low woody tonal swell
- near-silence into final frame

Do not use generic trailer music.

## Post-production rule

Do not rely on generative typography for the final brand lockup. If the bottle label or campaign typography is unstable, composite a clean official product image and add typography in post.
